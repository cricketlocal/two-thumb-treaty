How to play

  1. Two people hold one phone (or lay it flat between you).
  2. LEFT only touches the left half of the screen.
  3. RIGHT only touches the right half.
  4. Each mode gives opposite goals — that’s the treaty.
  5. Best of 3. Rematch or switch sides after every round.
  6. House rule: no covering your opponent’s half. Honour system.
